One man running the whole company - including the parts a system should run.

Murrieta Solar Power has a genuine local identity: a hometown installer that gives back to local schools and teams, with solar electric, battery, and solar pool heating all under one roof. But the operation runs entirely through Jeff. The site is a GoDaddy builder page with placeholder imagery and an unfinished nav item, there is no quote form or financing calculator, and a CRM already paid for is sitting with 20 leads never followed up. The bottleneck is not demand or reputation. It's that every job funnels through one person's inbox and memory.

Solar is a considered, high-ticket purchase, homeowners take days or weeks to decide, and a slow follow-up is the single biggest reason a warm lead goes to a competitor instead. Twenty leads sitting untouched in a CRM that is already paid for is not a marketing problem, it's revenue already earned and not yet collected. Fixing the follow-up loop on those twenty leads alone is likely worth more, faster, than any new ad spend - and it needs zero new traffic to prove it.

The CRM is already bought. The site already has real services and a real local identity. What's missing is the system that runs both without needing Jeff to remember everything.

Priority Roadmap - Fix First
  • Work the 20 stale leads: a follow-up sequence through the CRM you already own
  • A real quote form + financing calculator on the site, not a phone-only funnel
  • Automated speed-to-lead: instant reply and drip follow-up on every new enquiry
  • Fix the unfinished build: replace AI/placeholder imagery, remove the blank nav page
Build Next - Compounding Growth
  • Review engine: turn finished installs into named, dated, star-rated proof
  • License and certification front and centre - a trust signal solar buyers specifically look for
  • Local SEO: schema, canonical tags, service-area pages for Murrieta and Temecula Valley
  • Ads once the funnel is proven, so every new lead lands in a system that actually works it

The leads already exist. The system to work them doesn't.
