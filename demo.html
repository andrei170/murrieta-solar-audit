Common questions

Before you ask, a few answers.

How long does installation take?

Timelines vary by system size and permitting in your city — this gets confirmed once your specific site assessment is done, not guessed upfront.

Do I need to replace my roof first?

Not always. It depends on your roof's age and condition, which is part of what the on-site assessment covers before any install is scheduled.

What happens if my system needs a repair later?

Solar Electric Repair is one of our standing services — existing systems, including ones we didn't originally install, can be diagnosed and fixed.

Is the online estimate accurate?

It's a real range based on what you tell us, not a placeholder number — the final quote gets refined once we've seen your roof and usage.
