Financing

Ways to pay for a system that pays you back.

Every household's situation is different — this page lays out the common paths, not a one-size answer.

Cash Purchase

Own the system outright from day one, no monthly payment, the fastest way to the full return on investment.

Solar Loan

Spread the cost over time while still owning the system, often structured so payments track close to a prior utility bill.

Lease / PPA

Lower upfront cost, someone else owns the equipment, and you pay for the power it produces instead.

On tax credits and incentives:

Federal and state solar incentives change over time, and eligibility depends on your specific situation. Rather than quote a figure here that may be out of date by the time you read it, we'll walk through exactly what applies to your household on your quote call — and recommend confirming anything tax-related with your own accountant or tax professional before you rely on it.
